# Instruments

Phone-first web instruments. Open them on an iPhone from GitHub Pages: https://shawnchiki.github.io/instruments/

## Sunspot (`sunspot/`)

A voice-chop arpeggiator made for DAFA NATION, styled after their black sun imagery.

1. **Voice tab:** record up to 30 seconds of talking, singing or rapping, or upload your own audio (or a video's sound). The recording is cut at every transient, and each chop becomes a dot. Left to right is time and low to high is pitch. Filled suns have a clear pitch; rings are breaths, hits and S sounds.
2. **Hold a finger near the dots** and the arpeggiator cycles through the nearest ones. Two fingers play two clusters. Hold keeps it going after you let go.
3. **Tone tab:** the Tone dial fades from raw voice to resonators ringing at each chop's note. Snap retunes the chops to a key, and "Find key from voice" picks the key for you.
4. **Effects pad** (turn it on in the Space tab): a second pad on the bottom third. Up and down changes speed and pitch, left is stutter and right plays full chops. It stays where you leave it; tap the middle dot to reset.
5. **Synth tab:** a synth doubles every chop's note (Glass, Saw or Sub).
6. **Saves tab:** save the current voice with all its settings, then **Load** it later, or use **Settings** to copy just the knobs onto a new voice. "Turn all effects off" goes back to the raw voice.
7. **Red button:** records your jam to share.

Effects (Tone, Snap, Synth, Echo, Reverb) start off.

The last recording is kept on the phone between visits.

## Substellar (`substellar/`)

A phat mono synth in the style of the Moog Sub Phatty, dressed in psychedelic deep space. Best with the phone turned sideways.

1. **Play the keys** at the bottom. Slide your finger for legato runs. OCT UP / DOWN shift the range, and HOLD keeps the last note ringing while you turn knobs.
2. **BEND** springs back to the middle. **MOD** stays where you leave it and adds LFO wobble.
3. **Left rail tabs:** Oscillators (two morphing waves, 16'–2' octaves, detune, hard sync), Mixer (osc 1, osc 2, sub, noise), Ladder Filter (cutoff, resonance that self-oscillates, drive, envelope amount, key track, 6/12/18/24 dB slope), Envelopes (filter and volume ADSR), LFO Wobble (six shapes up to 100 Hz, sent to pitch, filter or wave), Play (glide, legato, bend range, volume, MIDI), Space (ping-pong echo and cosmic reverb), and Sounds (10 presets plus BIG BANG random).
4. **Knobs:** drag up or right to turn up. Double-tap to reset.
5. **On a computer:** keys A–K play, Z / X change octave, and a MIDI keyboard works in Chrome (CC1 = mod, CC74 = cutoff, CC71 = resonance).

Your last sound is kept on the device between visits.

## Grist (`grist/`)

A two-finger granulator for your voice. Turn the phone sideways: the whole screen is your recording.

1. **Voice tab:** record up to 30 seconds, upload audio, or try the demo voice. The white lines are the chops, cut at every hit and syllable.
2. **Hold two fingers on the wave.** The left finger is the start of the loop and the right finger is the end. Slide the left finger up for a longer fade in, and the right finger up for a longer fade out.
3. **Add a third finger anywhere** to bend the pitch: middle of the screen is normal, up is higher, down is lower. Let go and it springs back with a wobble.
4. **Lift one finger** and its edge stays put, so you can move the other one on its own. **One finger alone** scrubs a frozen cloud of grains at that spot.
5. **Magnet** (rail) snaps your fingers onto the chops. **Lock** (rail) keeps the sound going after you let go.
6. **Fingers tab:** change what each finger's up/down does (fade in, fade out, pitch, grain size, spray, tone, speed), the pitch range, swoops vs notes vs pentatonic, and whether pitch springs back.
7. **Grains tab:** loop through the window or scatter in it, speed, reverse, grain size, density, spray, tone. **Space tab:** echo and reverb (off to start).

On a computer, click and drag across the wave to set a window. The last recording is kept on the device between visits.
