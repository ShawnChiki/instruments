# Instruments

Phone-first web instruments. Open them on an iPhone from GitHub Pages: https://shawnchiki.github.io/instruments/

## Sunspot (`sunspot/`)

A voice-chop arpeggiator made for DAFA NATION, styled after their black sun imagery.

1. **Voice tab:** record up to 12 seconds of talking, singing or rapping, or load an audio file. The recording is cut at every transient, and each chop becomes a dot. Left to right is time and low to high is pitch. Filled suns have a clear pitch; rings are breaths, hits and S sounds.
2. **Hold a finger near the dots** and the arpeggiator cycles through the nearest ones. Two fingers play two clusters. Hold keeps it going after you let go.
3. **Tone tab:** the Tone dial fades from raw voice to resonators ringing at each chop's note. Snap retunes the chops to a key, and "Find key from voice" picks the key for you.
4. **Synth tab:** a synth doubles every chop's note (Glass, Saw or Sub).
5. **Red button:** records your jam to share.

The last recording is kept on the phone between visits.

## Substellar (`substellar/`)

A phat mono synth in the style of the Moog Sub Phatty, dressed in psychedelic deep space. Best with the phone turned sideways.

1. **Play the keys** at the bottom. Slide your finger for legato runs. OCT UP / DOWN shift the range, and HOLD keeps the last note ringing while you turn knobs.
2. **BEND** springs back to the middle. **MOD** stays where you leave it and adds LFO wobble.
3. **Left rail tabs:** Oscillators (two morphing waves, 16'–2' octaves, detune, hard sync), Mixer (osc 1, osc 2, sub, noise), Ladder Filter (cutoff, resonance that self-oscillates, drive, envelope amount, key track, 6/12/18/24 dB slope), Envelopes (filter and volume ADSR), LFO Wobble (six shapes up to 100 Hz, sent to pitch, filter or wave), Play (glide, legato, bend range, volume, MIDI), Space (ping-pong echo and cosmic reverb), and Sounds (10 presets plus BIG BANG random).
4. **Knobs:** drag up or right to turn up. Double-tap to reset.
5. **On a computer:** keys A–K play, Z / X change octave, and a MIDI keyboard works in Chrome (CC1 = mod, CC74 = cutoff, CC71 = resonance).

Your last sound is kept on the device between visits.
