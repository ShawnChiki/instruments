# Instruments

Phone-first web instruments. Open them on an iPhone from GitHub Pages: https://shawnchiki.github.io/instruments/

## Sunspot (`sunspot/`)

A voice-chop arpeggiator made for DAFA NATION, styled after their black sun imagery.

1. **Voice tab:** record up to 12 seconds of talking, singing or rapping, or load an audio file. The recording is cut at every transient, and each chop becomes a dot. Left to right is time and low to high is pitch. Filled suns have a clear pitch; rings are breaths, hits and S sounds.
2. **Hold a finger near the dots** and the arpeggiator cycles through the nearest ones. Two fingers play two clusters. Hold keeps it going after you let go.
3. **Tone tab:** the Tone dial fades from raw voice to resonators ringing at each chop's note. Snap retunes the chops to a key, and "Find key from voice" picks the key for you.
4. **Synth tab:** a synth doubles every chop's note (Glass, Saw or Sub).
5. **Red button:** records your jam to share.

The last recording is kept on the phone between visits.
